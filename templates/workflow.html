How we work

Cryo-EM workflow

From sample preparation to structure determination, our team supports every stage of your project.

ACCEM process

A clear path from sample to structure

Each project is planned with the facility team to protect sample quality, use microscope time efficiently, and deliver reliable structural data.

Start a request
01
Step one

Sample submission

Share your sample details, purification data, buffer conditions, and the experiment you want to perform. The facility team reviews suitability before scheduling.

Planning & consultation
02
Step two

Grid freezing

Samples are vitrified on EM grids using controlled temperature, humidity, blotting, and plunge-freezing conditions.

Vitrification
03
Step three

Grid clipping and insertion

Vitrified grids are clipped, inspected, and carefully inserted into cartridges for safe transfer to the microscope.

Grid handling
04
Step four

Grid screening

Grids are screened to assess ice thickness, particle distribution, aggregation, and sample quality before high-resolution collection.

Quality check
05
Step five

Data collection

High-resolution movies are acquired on the 200 kV Talos Arctica using dose-fractionation and the Gatan K2 direct electron detector.

Microscope session
06
Step six

Data processing

Movies are transferred securely for motion correction, CTF estimation, particle picking, classification, and reconstruction workflows.

Computing & analysis
07
Step seven

Structure determination

Refinement and interpretation lead to a high-resolution 3D structure and the scientific insight your project set out to discover.

Final structure
